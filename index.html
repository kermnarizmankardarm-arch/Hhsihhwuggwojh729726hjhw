II

Choose your methodology

Three ways to interrogate an image. Each one sends a different instruction to the vision model — and each returns a prompt with a different shape. Pick one; the whole forge changes colour around it.

IWP

Imperative
Weighted Prompting

Terse directives, numerically weighted. Maximum control, minimum prose.

The model is ordered, not asked. Every concept arrives as a command with a number attached, so the weight of each idea is explicit and tunable.

crimson rim light (1.4), 35mm, shallow depth (1.2),
wet asphalt reflections (0.9) --ar 2:3 --style raw
NLDP

Natural Language
Descriptive Prompting

A cinematic paragraph. The image described as a story a camera could shoot.

The model is asked to narrate. Light, lens, weather and mood come back as flowing sentences, which diffusion models handle with surprising subtlety.

A lone figure waits on a rain-slick platform at dusk;
low sodium light blooms through the haze, 50mm, film grain…
SSP

Structured
Schema Prompting

A machine-readable blueprint. Every attribute its own labelled field.

The model fills a form. Lighting, lens, palette, composition and mood each get their own field, which makes the result trivial to edit by hand.

{
  "subject": "figure at window",
  "key_light": "north window, 5600K",
  "lens": "85mm f/1.8",
  "mood": "quiet, contemplative"
}

←→ to move between cards, Enter to select. Your choice is remembered on this device.
